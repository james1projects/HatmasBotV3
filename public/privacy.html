PRIVACY

Privacy

Effective July 10, 2026

The short version: logging in stores your public identity in a signed cookie in your own browser and nothing else. This site never stores your passwords or access tokens, never sees your card number, and runs no ads, analytics, or trackers.

Logging in with Twitch

When you log in with Twitch, this site asks Twitch for your public identity only — no permissions on your account (the OAuth request carries zero scopes). Your Twitch user id, username, display name, and avatar URL are placed in a signed cookie in your browser. That cookie is the entire record of your login: there is no server-side session, and the access token Twitch returns is used once to read your identity and immediately discarded.

Logging in with YouTube (Google user data)

When you log in with YouTube, this site requests the read-only youtube.readonly permission and uses it exactly once: a single API call to learn your own channel's id, name, and thumbnail, so your share portfolio can be found. The Google access token is then discarded — it is never stored, and no other data is read from your account. Your channel id, name, and thumbnail go into the same kind of signed browser cookie as a Twitch login.

Hatmaster.tv's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Linking your YouTube channel to your Twitch account

If you choose to link the two, this site stores one record: your YouTube channel id next to your Twitch username, so shares earned by your YouTube comments land in your Twitch portfolio. That is the only thing the link stores.

What the market keeps

The Hatmas Market is a game, and game state is kept server-side: share holdings, trades, dividends, god nominations, and requests, keyed to your Twitch username or YouTube channel id. Usernames and holdings appear on public leaderboards — you can hide yourself with the visibility toggle on your portfolio page. Hat balances live in the streaming tooling, keyed to your Twitch username.

Payments

The $5 priority god request is processed entirely by Stripe on their hosted checkout page. This site never sees or stores your card details. It keeps what fulfillment needs: the god you chose, the Twitch username you entered, your optional message, the amount, and Stripe's receipt id — used to queue your request and to issue refunds.

Cookies

  • hatmas_session — your signed login identity, expires after 30 days. Logging out deletes it.
  • hatmas_oauth_state and hatmas_oauth_link — short-lived (10 minute) security cookies that only exist during a login or account-link round trip.

That's all of them. No advertising, analytics, or third-party tracking cookies.

Removing your data

Want your portfolio, transactions, account link, or payment records deleted, or your YouTube channel unlinked? Whisper Hatmaster on Twitch, or use the contact on the community page, and it will be removed. Logging out already removes the only login record that exists — the cookie in your browser. You can also revoke this site's YouTube access any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions (it holds no standing access, but revoking is your right).

Changes
